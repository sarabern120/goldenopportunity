Privacy Policy (Click to view / Expand)

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

1. Data Controller

This Mobile Application and website are operated by Golden Opportunity AB, based in Sweden.

Contact / Data Protection Lead: Sara Bern
Email: sara@goldenopportunity.se

2. Scope and Supported Languages

This Privacy Policy applies to the GO EFT Tapping mobile application available on iOS and Android. The application interface and audio guidance support 4 languages: Swedish, English, Arabic, and Ukrainian.

3. Processing of Session Data (Health Data)

  • Data Processing: During a tapping round, the app processes user spoken inputs or health assessments solely to facilitate the session.
  • Zero-Storage Architecture: Audio data and session details remain exclusively in temporary volatile memory (RAM). All spoken data and session history are immediately deleted upon leaving or closing the app.
  • No Server Uploads: We do not record, store, or transmit your voice or personal health data to external cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not collect, share, or sell user personal data, analytics, or health information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Rights Under GDPR

Because no personal health data is saved on servers or devices after session completion, no persistent tracking takes place. European users maintain all rights under the GDPR. For inquiries, contact sara@goldenopportunity.se.
